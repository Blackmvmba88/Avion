# Avion Roadmap 2025

## Vision

Transform Avion into a comprehensive, accessible flight simulation platform that balances realism with playability, while supporting multiple platforms and fostering an active community.

## Risk Management

### Technical Risks
- **Physics complexity**: Phased implementation, iterative testing
- **Performance**: Profiling, optimization passes
- **Cross-platform issues**: Regular testing on target devices

### Schedule Risks
- **Feature creep**: Strict phase planning, prioritization
- **Resource constraints**: Focus on core features first
- **Dependencies**: Minimal external dependencies

### Market Risks
- **Competition**: Unique features, community focus
- **User adoption**: Marketing, tutorials, demos
- **Retention**: Regular updates, compelling gameplay
